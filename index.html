HomeDoodle

Doodle on your friends' faces, live.

HomeDoodle is a video chat for up to 4 people on the same Wi-Fi. Draw on each other's faces and stick on hats, glasses and mustaches that follow your head as you move.

Private by design. Your video goes straight between the phones in the room, over your Wi-Fi, encrypted. Nothing is recorded or saved, and there are no accounts.

The app is free, with a short kid-safe ad before a room starts.
